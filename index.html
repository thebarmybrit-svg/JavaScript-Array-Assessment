Attach Emails to Images

  • Instructions
  • Generate random image, either by refreshing the page or clicking the "new random image" button.
  • Fill out a valid email in the form below, then click the "link email" button. You may link multiple emails to the same image, but you cannot link duplicate images to emails.
  • The valid email will be successfully linked to the image and displayed below. If the email submitted is the same as a previously submitted email, the image will be added to the existing email, not creating a new section.
  • You may click on the images below to get a ful view of the image, and can exit the view either by clicking off the image or the x on the top right.
  • You may delete any images in the stored sections, detatching them from the email and removed from the array, by clicking the red button in the top right corner. If this results in the last image attached to the email removed, the saved email address is also removed.
